What is Leadership & Management?

Leadership = guiding people toward a shared goal with clarity, trust, and support. A new manager holds weekly 1:1s, sets clear goals, and celebrates wins, and the team's turnover drops.

What you'll learn

Feedback

  • SBI model: Describe the Situation, the Behavior, and its Impact.
  • Timely: Feedback works best soon after the event.
  • Specific praise: Name exactly what was good so people repeat it.

Delegation

  • Delegate outcomes: Hand off the goal and the ownership, not just step-by-step instructions.
  • Match the support: Adjust guidance to each person's experience and motivation.
  • Check-ins, not micromanaging: Agree on review points, then step back.

Motivation & trust

  • Autonomy, mastery, purpose: Three big drivers of motivation: control over your work, getting better, and meaning.
  • Psychological safety: When a team feels safe to speak up, ask questions, and admit mistakes.
  • Recognition: Showing people their work is seen and valued.

Decisions & change

  • Reversible vs irreversible: Move fast on easy-to-undo decisions; slow down on hard-to-undo ones.
  • Disagree and commit: Debate openly, then fully support the final decision.
  • Explain the why: People accept change more easily when they understand the reason.
