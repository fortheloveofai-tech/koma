What is Project Management?

Project management = planning, organizing, and guiding work so a team delivers a specific goal on time and on budget. Launching an app in 3 months: break it into tasks, assign owners, track progress weekly, and fix delays early.

What you'll learn

Scope & planning

  • Scope: The defined work and deliverables of the project.
  • Scope creep: Additions to scope without adjusting time or budget.
  • WBS: Work breakdown structure: splitting the project into smaller, manageable pieces.

Scheduling

  • Milestone: A key checkpoint with zero duration.
  • Dependency: A task that can't start until another one finishes.
  • Critical path: The longest chain of dependent tasks. A delay here delays the whole project.

Agile & Scrum

  • Sprint: A short, fixed cycle (often 2 weeks) that delivers working results.
  • Backlog: A prioritized list of work still to do.
  • Stand-up: A short daily meeting: what I did, what I'll do, what's blocking me.

Risk & stakeholders

  • Risk register: A list of possible problems with their likelihood, impact, and response plan.
  • Risk vs issue: A risk might happen; an issue is happening now.
  • Stakeholder updates: Regular updates tailored to each audience.
