What is Generative AI & Prompting?

Generative AI = models that create new text, images, or code by learning patterns from huge amounts of data. You type “Write a polite email asking my landlord to fix the heater” and an AI drafts it in seconds.

What you'll learn

How LLMs work

  • Tokens: Chunks of text (often parts of words) that the model reads and writes.
  • Next-token prediction: The model picks a likely next token based on patterns from training, again and again.
  • Context window: How much text the model can consider at once: your prompt plus the conversation.

Prompt engineering

  • Role + context: Tell the AI who it is and what situation you're in.
  • Few-shot examples: Show one or two examples of the output you want; the model copies the pattern.
  • Constraints & format: Say the length, tone, and format you need.

Hallucinations & safety

  • Hallucination: When AI states something false or made-up as if it were true.
  • Verification: Check important facts, numbers, and quotes against trusted sources.
  • Data privacy: Don't paste secrets or personal data into tools that may store or learn from it.

AI at work

  • Copilots: AI built into tools you already use, to draft, summarize, or code alongside you.
  • AI agents: AI that plans steps and uses tools (search, files, apps) to finish a task.
  • Human in the loop: A person reviews or approves important AI actions before they happen.
