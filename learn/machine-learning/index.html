What is Machine Learning?

Machine learning = teaching computers to find patterns in data so they can make predictions without being explicitly programmed. Your email app learns from millions of examples to spot spam, without anyone writing a rule for every scam.

What you'll learn

Supervised learning

  • Label: The correct answer attached to each training example.
  • Classification: Predicting a category.
  • Regression: Predicting a number.

Unsupervised learning

  • Clustering: Grouping similar items together without predefined labels.
  • Anomaly detection: Spotting data points that don't fit the normal pattern.
  • Dimensionality reduction: Squeezing many columns into a few that keep most of the information.

Training & evaluation

  • Train/test split: Hold back part of the data to test the model on examples it never saw.
  • Overfitting: The model memorizes the training data, noise included, and fails on new data.
  • Precision: Of everything the model flagged, how much was truly positive.

Neural networks

  • Neuron: A tiny unit that weighs its inputs, adds them up, and passes a result on.
  • Layers: Neurons stacked in layers. Early layers learn simple patterns; deeper ones learn complex ones.
  • Weights: The adjustable numbers the network tunes during training to reduce its errors.
