What is Public Speaking?

Public speaking = communicating a clear message to an audience so they understand it, remember it, and act on it. Instead of 30 dense slides, a speaker tells one customer story, makes 3 key points, and ends with one clear ask.

What you'll learn

Structure

  • One big idea: Every talk needs one core message you can say in a sentence.
  • Rule of three: People remember three points better than seven.
  • Call to action: End by telling the audience exactly what to do next.

Storytelling

  • Story arc: Situation → challenge → turning point → result.
  • Specific details: Names, numbers, and moments make stories vivid.
  • Data + story: Pair a number with a human example.

Delivery

  • Pause: A short silence before or after a key point adds weight and replaces filler words.
  • Eye contact: Look at one person for a sentence or so to connect.
  • Pace & volume: Vary your speed and loudness to hold attention.

Nerves & Q&A

  • Rehearse out loud: Practicing aloud, ideally standing, builds real fluency.
  • Reframe anxiety: Nervous energy feels a lot like excitement. Calling it excitement can help.
  • Handling Q&A: Listen fully, repeat the question, answer briefly, and admit when you don't know.
