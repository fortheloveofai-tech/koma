What is Entrepreneurship?

Entrepreneurship = finding a real problem, building a solution people will pay for, and turning it into a business. A dog walker notices clients want photo updates, adds them, and raises prices 20% without a single complaint.

What you'll learn

Idea validation

  • Problem-solution fit: Evidence that a real group has the problem and wants your kind of fix.
  • Customer discovery: Interviews about people's current behavior and pains, not pitching your idea.
  • Pre-sales: Asking people to pay or commit before you build.

MVP & iteration

  • MVP: The smallest product that tells you whether customers want it.
  • Iterate: Improve in small cycles based on feedback and data.
  • Pivot: Change strategy when the evidence says the original plan won't work.

Business model

  • Revenue model: How you get paid: subscription, one-time sale, ads, commission, and so on.
  • Unit economics: Profit or loss per customer: lifetime value vs. cost to acquire.
  • Gross margin: (Revenue − cost of goods) ÷ revenue.

Customers & funding

  • Niche first: Start with a narrow group you can serve really well.
  • Bootstrapping: Funding the business from your own money and revenue.
  • Venture capital: Investors fund high-growth startups in exchange for equity (ownership).
