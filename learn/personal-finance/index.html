What is Personal Finance?

Personal finance = managing your money (earning, spending, saving, investing, protecting) so it supports the life you want. Someone taking home $4,000 a month sends $400 to savings automatically on payday, before spending anything.

What you'll learn

Budgeting

  • 50/30/20 rule: A simple split: 50% needs, 30% wants, 20% savings and debt payments.
  • Fixed vs variable: Fixed costs stay the same (rent); variable costs change (groceries).
  • Pay yourself first: Move savings out automatically on payday, before spending.

Emergency fund

  • Emergency fund: Cash for unexpected costs like a job loss or car repair.
  • 3–6 months: A common target: 3–6 months of essential expenses.
  • High-yield savings: A savings account that pays more interest while keeping money safe and reachable.

Debt & credit

  • APR: Annual percentage rate: the yearly cost of borrowing.
  • Avalanche vs snowball: Avalanche pays the highest-interest debt first (saves the most); snowball pays the smallest balance first (quick wins).
  • Credit score: Built by paying on time, keeping card balances low, and a long credit history.

Grow your money

  • Compound interest: Earning returns on your returns, so growth speeds up over time.
  • Retirement accounts: Tax-advantaged accounts, like a 401(k) or IRA in the US.
  • Inflation: Prices rise over time, so cash loses buying power.
