What is Digital Marketing?

Digital marketing = reaching the right people online, with the right message, at the right moment, and measuring what works. A coffee shop posts Reels, ranks on Google for “best latte near me”, emails regulars a weekly deal, then checks which one actually brought sales.

What you'll learn

SEO

  • Keywords & intent: The words people type, and why: are they learning, comparing, or buying?
  • On-page SEO: Put the main keyword in the title tag and H1, then answer the question better than anyone else.
  • Backlinks: Links from other sites act like votes of trust. One respected link beats 100 spammy ones.

Paid Ads

  • PPC: Pay-per-click: you pay the platform only when someone clicks your ad.
  • ROAS: Return on ad spend = revenue ÷ ad spend.
  • Retargeting: Showing ads to people who already visited your site but didn't buy.

Social Media

  • Organic vs paid: Organic = free posts that reach people naturally. Paid = ads and boosted posts.
  • Hook: The first 1–3 seconds of a post. If it doesn't stop the scroll, nothing else matters.
  • Engagement rate: Engagements (likes, comments, shares, saves) ÷ followers or reach.

Analytics

  • KPI: Key performance indicator: a number that shows progress toward a real goal.
  • CAC: Customer acquisition cost = marketing spend ÷ new customers.
  • UTM tags: Labels added to a link so analytics knows which campaign sent each visitor.
