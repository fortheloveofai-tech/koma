What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity = protecting systems, networks, and data from attacks, theft, and damage. Turning on two-factor authentication blocks most attackers, even if they steal your password.

What you'll learn

Phishing & social engineering

  • Phishing: Fake emails or texts that trick you into clicking, paying, or logging in.
  • Urgency & fear: Attackers rush you so you don't stop to think.
  • Check the sender: Look at the real email address and where links actually go before acting.

Passwords & MFA

  • Strong passwords: Long, unique, and hard to guess. A passphrase works well.
  • Password manager: An app that creates and stores a unique password for every site.
  • MFA: Multi-factor authentication: something you know plus something you have, like a phone code.

Malware & ransomware

  • Malware: Any software designed to harm, spy, or steal.
  • Ransomware: Malware that encrypts your files and demands payment to unlock them.
  • Patching: Installing updates that fix security holes.

Network security

  • Firewall: A filter that allows or blocks network traffic based on rules.
  • Encryption: Scrambling data so only someone with the key can read it.
  • Public Wi-Fi risk: Open networks can let attackers snoop, or fake the hotspot entirely.
