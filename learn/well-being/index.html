What is The Science of Well-Being?

Well-being science studies what actually makes people happier, and it's often not what we expect. Spending $20 on someone else tends to lift your mood more than spending it on yourself, studies suggest.

What you'll learn

Mind traps

  • Hedonic adaptation: We quickly get used to changes, good or bad, and drift back to our usual mood.
  • Social comparison: Comparing ourselves to others, especially online, can lower satisfaction.
  • Experiences over things: Spending on experiences often brings longer-lasting happiness than buying objects.

Daily habits

  • Gratitude practice: Regularly writing down a few things you're grateful for.
  • Acts of kindness: Small, helpful actions for others.
  • Savoring: Slowing down to fully enjoy good moments.

Sleep & movement

  • Sleep: Most adults need about 7–9 hours. Poor sleep affects mood, focus, and stress.
  • Movement: Regular physical activity is linked to better mood and less stress.
  • Consistent routine: Going to bed and waking at similar times helps sleep quality.

Mindfulness & stress

  • Mindfulness: Paying attention to the present moment without judging it.
  • Box breathing: Breathe in for 4, hold 4, out 4, hold 4. Repeat.
  • Reach out: Talking to someone you trust, or a professional, when things feel heavy.
