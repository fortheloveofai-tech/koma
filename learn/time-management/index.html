What is Time Management & Productivity?

Time management = choosing what matters most and protecting the time and focus to do it. Blocking 9–11 AM for deep work, with notifications off, finally finishes a report that kept slipping for two weeks.

What you'll learn

Prioritization

  • Eisenhower matrix: Sort tasks by urgent vs. important: do, schedule, delegate, or delete.
  • 80/20 rule: Often a small share of efforts drives most of the results.
  • MITs: Most important tasks: pick 1–3 to finish each day before anything else.

Deep focus

  • Deep work: Long, uninterrupted focus on demanding tasks.
  • Context switching: Jumping between tasks costs time and attention every time you switch.
  • Pomodoro: 25 minutes of focus, then a 5-minute break. Repeat.

Beating procrastination

  • Two-minute rule: If it takes less than 2 minutes, do it now.
  • Break it down: Shrink big, vague tasks into tiny, concrete first steps.
  • Emotion, not laziness: We often avoid tasks that feel uncertain, boring, or scary.

Systems & habits

  • Capture: Write every task and idea in one trusted place so your brain can focus.
  • Weekly review: Once a week, clear your inbox, check goals, and plan the week ahead.
  • Habit stacking: Attach a new habit to one you already have.
