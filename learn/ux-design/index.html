What is UX Design?

UX design = shaping the whole experience of using a product so it's useful, easy, and enjoyable. A checkout that asks for 3 fields instead of 12 gets far more people to finish buying.

What you'll learn

User research

  • User interview: A one-on-one conversation to understand someone's goals and pain points.
  • Open questions: Questions that invite stories, not yes/no answers.
  • Behavior over opinion: What people do matters more than what they say they'd do.

Structure & flows

  • Information architecture: How content is organized, labeled, and connected.
  • User flow: The steps a user takes to complete a goal.
  • Card sorting: Users group topics into categories, showing how they think.

Wireframes & prototypes

  • Wireframe: A low-detail sketch of a screen's layout, without colors or final content.
  • Prototype: A clickable mock-up that simulates the product.
  • Fidelity: How detailed a design is: low (sketch) to high (pixel-perfect).

Usability & accessibility

  • Usability testing: Watching users attempt real tasks with your design while they think aloud.
  • Contrast & text size: Enough color contrast and readable text help everyone, especially people with low vision.
  • Not color alone: Pair color with text or icons so colorblind users get the message too.
